# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small and medium business owners worldwide (often non-technical) searching for web development, SEO, content creation, ecommerce, and social media management help. They land on a service page from search or social, are evaluating credibility quickly, and want a low-friction way to start a conversation (message, free quote, or call) rather than fill out a long form.

## Product Purpose

Digitelle Studios' marketing site (digitelle-studios.com) is the storefront for its web development, SEO, content creation, ecommerce, and social media management services. Each service page (e.g. web-development-services.html) needs to build credibility fast and convert visitors into direct messages/bookings via the contact modal, WhatsApp/chat, or email. Success = more qualified messages and booked calls, especially for the web development offer.

## Brand Personality

Modern and bold — confident, contemporary, and capable, like a studio that builds great products (not a template reseller). Should feel handcrafted and current, with enough warmth that messaging the studio feels easy and low-risk.

## Anti-references

- The generic "AI SaaS landing page" template look: tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section, numbered 01/02/03 process steps used purely as scaffolding, gradient-text headings, identical icon+heading+text card grids repeated section after section, purple/blue gradient hero with blurred blobs.
- Cookie-cutter Bootstrap-starter-theme feel — should not look like an unmodified template.

## Design Principles

- Lead with proof, not claims: real client logos, portfolio work, and concrete outcomes carry more weight than generic feature lists.
- Every section should make it effortless to message or book — conversion paths are a first-class design concern, not an afterthought.
- Consistency across service pages: web development, SEO, ecommerce, content, and social pages should share one visual system, not diverge per page.
- Bold and current without sacrificing credibility — confidence expressed through typography, color, and layout choices, not through SaaS clichés.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA: body text contrast ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. All interactive elements (buttons, modal, accordion, nav) must be keyboard operable. Any scroll/entrance animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
